# `idea.md` artifact contract

Write a concise, decision-complete framing of the requested change.

```text
# Idea
## Outcome
## Users and context
## In scope
## Out of scope
## Success signals
## Open decisions
```

Keep every required section non-empty. Describe the product problem and desired
outcome without prescribing implementation. Do not hide unresolved product
decisions in assumptions, placeholders, or invented defaults.
