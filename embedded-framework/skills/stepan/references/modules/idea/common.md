# Idea shared rules

Apply these rules whenever authoring or consuming `idea.md`:

- treat `request.md`, completed clarifications, and approved declared inputs as
  the durable product context;
- do not infer users, scope, priorities, constraints, or success criteria when
  they could change the product meaning;
- preserve explicit user decisions and distinguish them from unresolved
  questions;
- keep the framing implementation-neutral and traceable to the original
  request.
- once approved, treat `idea.md` as the binding product-framing contract for
  requirements, design, review, and planning; downstream roles may use its
  request and clarification evidence to verify traceability but may not
  reinterpret the approved framing silently.
