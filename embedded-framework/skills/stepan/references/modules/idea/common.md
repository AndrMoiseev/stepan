# Idea shared rules

Apply these rules whenever authoring or consuming `idea.md`:

- Treat the approved idea as the framing contract for the change: its problem,
  goal, target user, expected outcome, and scope bound every downstream
  artifact.
- While authoring, treat only explicit user statements, recorded
  clarifications, and approved declared project inputs as product decisions.
  After approval, treat `idea.md` as the authoritative summary of those
  decisions for downstream work.
- Preserve the distinction between the current problem, the desired product
  outcome, and the proposed capability. Do not rewrite one as another merely to
  make the narrative smoother.
- Never silently broaden or narrow the target audience, outcome, included
  capability, or exclusions. Elaborate downstream behavior and design only
  within the approved framing.
- Keep the idea at product-framing level. Do not turn it into detailed
  requirements, acceptance scenarios, architecture, implementation steps, or a
  delivery plan.
- If declared inputs conflict or leave an ambiguity that could change the
  problem, goal, target user, expected outcome, or scope, do not choose a likely
  interpretation. Use the selected role's allowed blocking question or finding.
